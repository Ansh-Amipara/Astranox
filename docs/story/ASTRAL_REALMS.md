# The Astral Realms

**Astral Realms** are untamed, hidden sanctuary dimensions that overlap the world of Astranox. Rather than being battlegrounds or prisons, these realms serve as vast, natural ecosystems where wild Pokémon roam freely, stay, and live in harmony. They are invisible to ordinary people and can only be accessed by trainers who have proven their strength and rank.

## Realm Environment & Rules

* **Living Ecosystems:** Astral Realms are not just arenas; they are thriving, open environments where Pokémon stay, live, and interact naturally.
* **Thematic Typing:** Each realm focuses on **3 to 4 specific Pokémon types** based on its environment (e.g., an "Ancient Swamp" realm might only contain Grass, Water, and Bug types).
* **Capturable & Defeatable:** Pokémon in the Astral Realms can be battled normally. You can defeat them to earn Experience Points, or you can capture them to add to your collection.
* **Strict Rank Restrictions:** You may only enter realms that match your exact current Trainer Rank or below. For example, a **Gold Rank** trainer can only enter Gold, Silver, Bronze, or Black Iron realms. They **cannot** enter Platinum or higher realms under any circumstances.

## Example Realm Ecosystems

Each rank contains multiple themed realms to explore. Below are examples of how they are structured:

### Tier 1: Black Iron Rank Realms
1. **Lost Cave Realm** (Water / Rock / Ground)
2. **Dojo Realm** (Fighting / Normal)
3. **Venom Hive Realm** (Poison / Bug)

### Tier 2: Bronze Rank Realms
4. **Regi Ruins Realm** (Electric / Rock / Steel)
5. **Dragon Vault Realm** (Dragon / Fire)
6. **Honor Realm** (Grass / Fighting)

### Tier 3: Silver Rank Realms
7. **Life Shrine Realm** (Fairy / Psychic)
8. **Growth Shrine Realm** (Grass / Fairy)
9. **Muscle Jungle Realm** (Fighting / Bug / Grass)

*(Additional realms scale up through all 18 tiers, providing diverse ecosystems for every rank.)*
