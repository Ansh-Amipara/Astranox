# The Astral Exchange (Auction House)

The **Astral Exchange** is the premier economy hub in **Astranox**. Currently operating as an **Admin-Driven** auction house, it provides a secure marketplace where the server lists highly coveted loot, rare Pokémon, and exclusive items for players to bid on.

## Lore

Situated in the heart of the capital city, the Astral Exchange is managed by the **Golden Scales Syndicate**—a neutral faction of wealthy merchants and retired elite trainers. They recognized that the influx of rare artifacts and powerful Pokémon from the unstable Rifts required a regulated market. 

To ensure fairness, the Golden Scales employ powerful Psychic-type Pokémon to verify the authenticity of all goods and prevent fraudulent trades. While the main exchange operates strictly above board, whispers exist of a hidden "Black Market" dealing in forbidden corrupted materials.

---

## Core Features

The Astral Exchange operates completely asynchronously, allowing players to bid on server-listed items and Pokémon even while offline. Currently, players cannot list their own items.

### 1. Auction Types
* **Standard Bid:** The Admin sets a starting price and an auction duration (12, 24, or 48 hours). The highest bidder at the end of the duration wins.
* **Buyout (Fixed Price):** The Admin sets a hard price. The first buyer to pay this price instantly receives the item.
* **Mixed:** An auction with both a starting bid and a buyout limit. If the buyout price is met, the auction ends immediately.

### 2. Supported Currencies
The exchange supports multiple economic tiers, preventing inflation from ruining endgame trading.
* **Standard Money (₽):** Used for common items, low-tier Potential Crystals, and standard Pokémon.
* **Ancient Coins:** Used for high-tier evolution stones, rare cosmetics, and high-IV breeding stock.
* **Nova Coins:** The premium endgame currency used exclusively for trading Mythical/Eternal tier Pokémon, Legendary Fragments, and Realm Tickets.

## Tradable Goods

High-value, server-generated items are placed up for auction by the Zodiac Council (Admins), giving players a way to spend their wealth on exclusive items.

### Pokémon Auctions
* **Perfect IV Pokémon:** Admins list specially generated breeding stock with maxed IVs.
* **High Potential Pokémon:** Extremely rare Pokémon injected with Epic, Mythical, or Eternal potentials.
* **Shiny / Rare Variants:** Cosmetic variants and shiny Pokémon.

### Items and Materials
* **Upgrade Crystals:** IV Crystals and Potential Crystals (e.g., Diamond Crystals, Epic Crystals).
* **Rift Loot:** Astral Shards, Rift Essence, and Boss drops.
* **Consumables:** Rare Move Scrolls (TMs), specialized healing items, and Evolution Stones.

### Cosmetics and Mounts
* **Mount Skins:** Unused or duplicate mount drops from World Rift Bosses.
* **Trainer Outfits:** Rare clothing items and profile decorations.

## The Black Market (Special System)

Appearing only on weekends or during special server events, the **Black Market** is an NPC-driven branch of the Auction House. 

* **Location:** Randomly rotates between hidden alleys in major cities.
* **Function:** Sells incredibly rare, server-limited items (e.g., only 5 "Corrupted Auras" available per server).
* **Bidding:** Players bid blindly. When the timer expires, the top X bidders receive the item, and everyone else is refunded. 
* **Lore Tie-in:** The Golden Scales publicly disavow the Black Market, but secretly use it to offload highly unstable items recovered from Deep Rifts.

---

## Economy Sinks

Because the Auction House is currently admin-driven, it serves as the ultimate **gold sink** for the game. When players purchase items from the exchange, the currency is completely removed from the game, keeping inflation in check.

---

## Why this fits Astranox

This system acts as a perfect endgame wealth sink while providing exciting server events:

* **The Wealth Sink:** Players who grind Rift Dungeons or the Elite Infinite Tower accumulate massive amounts of currency. The Auction House gives them something highly exclusive to spend it on.
* **Server Events:** Admins can hype up the community by announcing special "Weekend Auctions" where a single, unique Corrupted Aura or Eternal Potential Pokémon is put up for bidding.
* **The Competitors:** PvP focused players can bypass the grind by saving up and purchasing the exact Pokémon and upgrade items they need directly from the server auctions.
