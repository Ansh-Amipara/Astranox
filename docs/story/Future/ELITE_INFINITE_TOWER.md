# Elite Infinite Tower

**Elite Infinite Tower** is an excellent endgame feature for **Astranox**. It gives max-level players a reason to keep improving their teams without affecting the main story.

## Lore

After the collision between the **Inner World** and the **Outer World**, a colossal tower emerged at the center of Lunaris. Unlike Astral Realms, the tower is not a natural realm—it is a fracture in reality where countless dimensions overlap.

The deeper you climb, the more unstable reality becomes. Every floor is generated differently, making no two runs the same.

---

## Entry Requirements

* Trainer Rank: **Diamond+** (or Legendary if you want it to be true endgame)
* At least **6 Pokémon**
* Daily attempts: **Unlimited** (or 3 per day if you want progression pacing)

---

## Tower Structure

* Endless Floors
* Every **10 Floors** = Mini Boss
* Every **25 Floors** = Elite Boss
* Every **50 Floors** = Realm Guardian
* Every **100 Floors** = Tower Overlord

Difficulty scales continuously.

---

## Random Modifiers

Each floor rolls 1–3 modifiers.

Examples:

* Pokémon recover only 50% HP after battle.
* Fire moves deal +30% damage.
* Weather is permanently Sandstorm.
* No healing items.
* Critical hit rate doubled.
* Speed reduced by 20%.
* Enemy gains random stat boost every 3 turns.
* Random Terrain every battle.

## Floor Rewards

Every floor grants:

* Trainer EXP
* Money
* Tower Tokens
* Pokémon EXP
* Small chance for rare items

Every boss floor grants:

* Rare Evolution Stones
* Rare Held Items
* Mythic Materials
* Astral Crystals
* Elite Keys

## Elite Tower Shop

Spend **Tower Tokens** on permanent upgrades.

Examples:

* Extra Healing Charges
* More Tower Revives
* Increased Reward Rate
* Increased Money Gain
* Increased Pokémon EXP
* Rare Item Drop Rate
* Additional Daily Attempt
* Cosmetic Titles
* Tower-exclusive Trainer Outfit
* Mount Skins

---

## Seasonal Leaderboard

Resets every month.

Rewards:

* Exclusive Trainer Title
* Profile Border
* Avatar Frame
* Mount Skin
* Legendary Cosmetic Effects
* Exclusive Badge

No gameplay advantages—cosmetics only.

---

## Endless Scaling

Enemy stats increase every floor.

Example:

* Floors 1–20 → +2% per floor
* Floors 21–50 → +4%
* Floors 51–100 → +6%
* Floors 100+ → +8%
* Floors 300+ → Special mechanics unlock

## Hardcore Mode

Optional challenge:

* No healing between floors.
* No revives.
* One run only.
* Higher rewards.
* Separate leaderboard.

---

### Ultimate Reward

If a player reaches milestone floors such as **100, 250, 500, 1000**, they unlock permanent achievements and cosmetics.

Example titles:

* Floor 100 — **Tower Challenger**
* Floor 250 — **Elite Conqueror**
* Floor 500 — **Reality Breaker**
* Floor 1000 — **Infinite Ascendant**

### Why this fits Astranox

This complements your existing systems well:

* **Astral Realms** focus on exploration and capturing Pokémon.
* **Rift Dungeons** provide handcrafted co-op or PvE challenges with Corrupted Pokémon that cannot be captured.
* **Elite Infinite Tower** becomes the ultimate solo endgame, testing team building, resource management, and strategic adaptation rather than simply increasing Pokémon levels. It creates long-term replayability without interfering with the story progression.
