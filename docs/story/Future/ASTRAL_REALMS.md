# The Astral Realms

**Astral Realms** are untamed, hidden sanctuary dimensions that overlap the world of Astranox. Rather than being battlegrounds or prisons, these realms serve as vast, natural ecosystems where wild Pokémon roam freely, stay, and live in harmony. They are invisible to ordinary people and can only be accessed by trainers who have proven their strength and rank.

## Realm Environment & Rules

* **Living Ecosystems:** Astral Realms are not just arenas; they are thriving, open environments where Pokémon stay, live, and interact naturally.
* **Thematic Typing:** Each realm focuses on **3 to 4 specific Pokémon types** based on its environment (e.g., an "Ancient Swamp" realm contains Grass, Water, Poison, and Bug types). This guarantees a wide variety of wild Pokémon spawns.
* **Capturable & Defeatable:** Pokémon in the Astral Realms can be battled normally. You can defeat them to earn Experience Points, or you can capture them to add to your collection.
* **No Bosses:** Astral Realms do not have guaranteed "Alpha Bosses." Every encounter is a purely wild encounter against the realm's inhabitants.
* **Strict Rank Restrictions:** You may only enter realms that match your exact current Trainer Rank or below. For example, an **E Rank** trainer can only enter E Rank and F Rank realms.

Instead of specific realms dictating the level and potential of Pokémon, the **Trainer Rank required** to enter the realm governs exactly how powerful the wild Pokémon will be. 

When you encounter a wild Pokémon, its Level is dynamically generated within the rank's strict level boundaries, and its Potential is dictated exactly by the realm tier you are exploring.

## Legendary Spawn Odds

Legendary, Mythical, and Super Legendary Pokémon appear exclusively as **ultra-rare wild spawns** in realms of S Rank and above. They are never guaranteed bosses. The mathematical odds of encountering one depend entirely on its classification tier.

The following odds represent the chance that any given wild encounter in an eligible realm will roll as the realm's featured Legendary species, rather than a regular habitat Pokémon.

| Legendary Tier | Encounter Odds | Percentage | Average Encounters | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 3** | 1 in 1,000 | 0.100% | 1,000 | The most accessible tier of Legendary Pokémon, forming the baseline for S Rank Origin Realms. |
| **Tier 2** | 1 in 4,000 | 0.025% | 4,000 | Considerably rarer, comparable to standard shiny hunting odds in traditional games. |
| **Tier 1** | 1 in 10,000 | 0.010% | 10,000 | Extremely elusive. Finding a Tier 1 world-defining Legendary is a major milestone. |
| **Super Legendary** | 1 in 32,768 | ~0.003% | 32,768 | Exclusive to SSS+ Rank Eternal Realms. The absolute rarest spawn possible in the entire game. |

> [!NOTE]
> If a realm features multiple Legendaries of the same tier (e.g., Latias and Latios in the Lakebound Sanctum), the successful Legendary spawn roll will randomly split between them (e.g., a 1/1,000 chance to hit the Tier 3 pool, followed by a 50/50 coin flip to determine which of the two eon Pokémon appears).
