# Rift Dungeons

## Lore

Thousands of years ago, the **Inner World (Astral Realms)** and the **Outer World (Lunaris)** collided for a brief moment.

The collision didn't fully merge the worlds.

Instead, it tore holes in reality.

These unstable fractures became known as **Rifts**.

Inside every Rift exists a broken pocket dimension where space, time, and nature are corrupted.

Normal Pokémon that wander inside slowly lose themselves and become **Corrupted Pokémon**.

These creatures cannot be caught because they no longer possess a stable soul.

When defeated, they dissolve into Astral energy and the Rift begins to stabilize.

---

# Corrupted Pokémon

These are NOT new Pokémon.

They're existing Pokémon with corruption.

Examples

* Corrupted Garchomp
* Corrupted Tyranitar
* Corrupted Gengar
* Corrupted Salamence
* Corrupted Metagross

Visual changes

* Black aura
* Purple energy cracks
* Red glowing eyes
* Shadow particles
* Distorted cries

Gameplay

* +20–50% stats
* Unique abilities
* More aggressive AI
* Exclusive corrupted moves

They **cannot be captured**.

When HP reaches 0:

> The corruption collapses into Astral Dust.

# Dungeon Types & Ranks

Rift Dungeons are categorized into 4 danger levels, scaling directly with the trainer's **Potential Rank**.

## Lesser Rift (Black Iron & Bronze)
* **Recommended Level:** 10–40
* **Floors:** 3
* **Bosses:** 1
* **Rewards:** Common Materials, Astral Shards

---

## Greater Rift (Silver, Gold, & Platinum)
* **Recommended Level:** 41–90
* **Floors:** 5
* **Mini Bosses:** 2
* **Rewards:** Rare Materials, Rift Essence

---

## Ancient Rift (Diamond & Epic)
* **Recommended Level:** 91–120
* **Floors:** 7
* **Boss:** Legendary Corrupted Pokémon
* **Rewards:** Epic Relics, Ancient Coins

---

## Cataclysm Rift (Legendary & Mythical)
* **Recommended Level:** 121–200
* **Floors:** 10 (Extremely difficult)
* **Boss:** World-Ending Threat
* **Rewards:** Mythical Drops, Nova Coins, Legendary Fragments

# Story Integration

These tie directly into the existing lore instead of making them a separate feature.

* **Astral Realms:** stable dimensions where Pokémon can be captured.
* **Lunaris:** the normal world.
* **Zodiac Council:** monitors cosmic balance.
* **Nine Thrones:** protect primordial forces.

The **Rifts** are **unstable tears** created by the ancient collision between the Astral Realms and Lunaris. Unlike Astral Realms—which remain intact and allow Pokémon to be captured—Rifts are broken, corrupted spaces that must be sealed. This fits naturally with the Zodiac Council's duty to monitor disturbances in the Astral Realms and gives them a compelling reason to recruit powerful trainers.

## Gameplay Loop Separation

This creates a clean separation between the PvE systems:

* **Wild Areas:** Catch normal Pokémon.
* **Astral Realms:** Catch rare and legendary Pokémon.
* **Rift Dungeons:** Fight corrupted, non-capturable Pokémon for progression materials and unique rewards.
* **League & NPCs:** Story progression.
* **World Boss Rifts:** Cooperative endgame events.

This gives Astranox distinct gameplay loops without overlapping their purposes, making the world feel much larger while keeping each activity meaningful.
